Wizard's Duel is a single-player console card game in C++ where you battle an AIwizard by strategically playing three card types: Creatures (summon units like Dragons and Golems with Attack/Health stats), Spells (one-time effects like Fireball for damage or Heal for recovery), and Artifacts (persistent upgrades like Mana Crystals).

Players gain +1 max mana each turn (starting at 1), using it to play cards—summoning up to 5 creatures, casting spells, or setting face-down Trapcards that trigger on opponent actions. Combat revolves around elemental strengths (Fire > Air > Earth > Water) and synergies (e.g., Fire+Air deals bonus damage).

The goal is to reduce the AI’s health to zero before yours reaches zero, with ties broken by active creatures and mana reserves. The game ends immediately when a player’s health depletes or the deck is exhausted, rewarding tactical card combos and mana management.
